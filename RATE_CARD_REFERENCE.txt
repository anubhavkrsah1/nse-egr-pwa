ANNUAL COMPLIANCE
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Rate cards must be:
  ✓ Approved by NSE Finance Committee
  ✓ Notified to depositors 30 days in advance
  ✓ Published on NSE website
  ✓ Logged with timestamp for audit
  ✓ Locked (no changes) during regulatory review

Process:
  1. Proposed rate change → Finance Committee approval
  2. 30-day notice to all active depositors (via SMS/Email)
  3. Effective date: First of next month
  4. Rate card locked (read-only) during review period
  5. Audit report generated for SEBI
